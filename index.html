Liber I
Overview
The plan, in brief.
The Program Upper/lower split, 4 sessions a week. Every muscle group twice. Same movements, different loading per person.
Reps Alex: heavier and fewer sets to hold strength on the cut. Chloe: more reps at moderate weight to build skill and muscle.
Protein Hit the number every day even when not hungry. Front-load earlier — 130g before 6 PM. Everything else can flex.
Deload Every 8–12 weeks, drop all weights by 40% for a week. Skip deloads and you stall, then break.

Liber V
Notes
Everything else worth writing down.
De Somno
Sleep
7–9 hours. Recovery, fat loss, and muscle growth all live here.
De Aqua
Water & Salt
Drink water all day. Add electrolytes on training days.
De Mensura
Tracking
Cronometer or MyFitnessPal for food. This logbook for weights.
De Libra
The Scale
Weigh every morning. Trust the weekly average, ignore daily numbers.
De Cursu
Cardio
Easy walks, 2–3 a week. Anything harder eats into recovery.
De Carne
Protein
Chicken, eggs, Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, beef, canned fish, shrimp, whey shakes. Front-load earlier in the day.
De Sociis
Together
Don't compare weights. Compete against your own last session.
De Medicina
Medical
Run targets by a doctor first. If energy crashes mid-session, eat more before changing anything else.
